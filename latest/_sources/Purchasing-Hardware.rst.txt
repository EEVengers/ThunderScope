Purchasing Hardware
===================

Both models of ThunderScope as well as our probe kit are available to be purchased at:

* `Crowd Supply <https://www.crowdsupply.com/eevengers/thunderscope>`_
* Mouser (Coming Soon)

Purchasing ThunderScope through our distributors helps fund work on future hardware 
as well as further development on the software the makes ThunderScope possible! 
In exchange, you get a calibrated unit that is guaranteed to be built to our spec, 
as well as support for the hardware should any issues arise with it. 

As for the probes, we just pack up a set of them from `Testec <https://testec.de>`_ 
(who make pretty nice probes!) and sell them at minimal margin (after the standard 30% 
for the distributor of course). The idea is to offer a set of good quality probes that 
exactly match the capabilities of ThunderScope, at a fairly competitive price. 
If you can find better ones for cheaper or already have probes kicking around, use those - 
ThunderScope is made to work with all standard oscilloscope probes!
